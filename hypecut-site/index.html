How it works

Three steps. Zero timelines.

No editing software, no scrubbing through 40 minutes of footage looking for the one good jump.

STEP 01

Upload gameplay

FAQ

Questions

What kind of footage works best?
Any screen recording of your game: playtests, stream VODs or a session you capture yourself. Longer recordings give us more moments to pick from. We recommend at least 10 minutes of real play.
Do I need any video editing skills?
No. You upload, pick how many clips you want and a caption style, and download finished vertical videos. You can tweak titles and trims if you want to, but you don't have to.
Can I post the clips on YouTube Shorts and TikTok?
Yes. Clips export at 9:16 with captions burned in and stay under 60 seconds, so they fit both platforms. You own the footage you upload and the clips we make from it.
How does Pro work, and can I cancel?
Monthly is $19.99 for 20 clips a month; yearly is $99.99 for 80 clips a month. Checkout is handled securely by Paddle (cards, PayPal and more, with taxes included), and you land back in the tool with Pro unlocked: no watermark, up to 8 clips per video, 60-second clips and extra caption styles. Your plan renews automatically, and if you run out of clips mid-project we automatically add 10 more for $4.99 (at most 5 times a month), so you're never stopped. You also get a Pro key to unlock other browsers. Cancel anytime with the "Manage subscription" button in the tool, and get a full refund within 14 days.
Which games does it work with?
Any game you can record on your computer. Hypecut works on the video file itself, so it doesn't need access to your game or account.
