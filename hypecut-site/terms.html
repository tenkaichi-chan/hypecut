What Hypecut is

Hypecut is a web tool that turns your gameplay recordings into short vertical clips. The tool runs in your web browser. Your videos are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to us.

Using the tool

  • Only upload recordings you have the right to use, such as gameplay of your own game or footage you recorded yourself.
  • You own the clips you make. We claim no rights to your videos or clips.
  • Don't use Hypecut to make content that is illegal, hateful, or that infringes someone else's rights.
  • Hypecut is an independent product and is not affiliated with any game or platform.

Free plan

The free plan lets you make 3 clips a month, with a small "made with hypecut" watermark. We may change free-plan limits at any time.
