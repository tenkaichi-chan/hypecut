Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version

Your videos never leave your device. The clip tool runs entirely in your browser. We only collect what's needed to run Pro subscriptions.

Your videos

Recordings you add to the tool are read and processed by your own browser. They are not uploaded to our servers. If you turn on speech subtitles, a speech-recognition model is downloaded to your browser from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and runs on your device; your audio is not sent anywhere.

Pro purchases

Payments are processed by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy; we never see your full card details. We keep your Paddle customer and subscription IDs linked to your Pro key so we can check that Pro is active, plus a count of how many Pro clips you've made this month. The key is also stored in your browser so Pro stays unlocked.

Hosting and cookies

The site is hosted by Netlify, which may log basic technical data (such as IP address) for security. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps small settings, such as how many free clips you've made this month and your Pro key.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you at any time by replying to your receipt email.

Children

Pro purchases are for adults or must be made with a parent or guardian's permission.

See also our Terms of Service and Refund Policy.
