How to turn horizontal gameplay into vertical 9:16 video

Updated 28 November 2026

Games are played on wide screens, but Shorts, TikTok and Reels are tall. A plain centre crop cuts off half the screen and often the action with it. Here are your options, and how to do it without an editor.

Step by step

  1. Drop in your 16:9 recording. Open ReelMyGame and drop in your gameplay file (MP4, MOV or WebM). It stays on your computer.
  2. Pick a crop style. Smart crop fills the whole 9:16 frame and moves to follow the action on screen. Full frame keeps the whole wide picture in the middle with a blurred copy filling the top and bottom, which is better for strategy games or busy HUDs.
  3. Add a caption. Each clip gets a hook caption you can edit before it is made.
  4. Download 1080x1920 MP4s. Clips come out at 1080x1920, the standard size for vertical video, ready to post.

Smart crop or full frame?

  • Smart crop works best when the action is in one place: shooters, platformers, fighting and racing games.
  • Full frame with blur works best when the whole screen matters: strategy, survival base-building or anything with a busy interface.

Doing it by hand

In a desktop editor you would set a 1080x1920 project, scale the footage, keyframe the position to follow the action, and render. That is fine for one clip and slow for ten. ReelMyGame does the crop, captions and export for every clip in one go.

Questions

What resolution are the clips?

1080x1920 (9:16), at 30 frames per second.

Will my HUD get cut off?

With smart crop, parts of the edge of the screen can be cut. Use full frame with blur if you need the whole screen visible.
