How to turn gameplay into YouTube Shorts (free, in your browser)

Updated 28 November 2026

A YouTube Short has to be vertical (9:16), under 60 seconds, and grab attention in the first second. Most gameplay is recorded wide (16:9) and runs for 20 minutes or more. Here is how to get from one to the other without opening a video editor.

Step by step

  1. Record a real session. Use any screen recorder, a playtest capture or a stream recording. Aim for at least 10 minutes of actual play: more footage means more moments to choose from. MP4, MOV and WebM files all work.
  2. Open the free tool and drop the file in. Go to ReelMyGame in Chrome or Edge on a computer and drop the recording onto the page. The file is read by your browser on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded, so a 5 GB recording starts as fast as a 50 MB one.
  3. Let it find the best moments. ReelMyGame scores every second of the recording by how loud it is and how much is moving on screen. Fights, explosions, close calls and shouting stand out as spikes, and the highest spikes become your clips.
  4. Check the crop and captions. Each clip is cropped to 9:16 around the action, or kept full frame with a blurred fill if you prefer. Each clip gets a hook caption you can edit, and you can turn on word-by-word speech subtitles (beta) if you talk over your gameplay.
  5. Download and post. Download the MP4s and upload them to YouTube Shorts as they are. Clips stay under 60 seconds, so they also fit TikTok and Instagram Reels.

What makes a gameplay Short work

  • Start on the action. Viewers decide in a second or two. Trim so the clip opens on the moment, not the build-up.
  • Keep it short. 15 to 30 seconds is plenty for one moment. One idea per Short.
  • Put words on screen. Many people scroll with the sound off. A short caption says what is happening.
  • Post often. Several Shorts a week from one recording beats one polished edit a month.

Why cut Shorts in the browser?

Desktop editors work, but cutting by hand means scrubbing through the whole recording, cropping each clip to vertical and typing captions one by one. Upload-based clip sites make you wait while gigabytes of footage go to someone else's server. ReelMyGame does the work on your own computer, so there is no upload, no queue and no copy of your footage anywhere else.

Free clips can be up to 45 seconds and Pro clips up to 60 seconds, which fits the YouTube Shorts limit.

Yes. It works on the video file, so any game you can record on your computer works. It never needs access to your game or account.
