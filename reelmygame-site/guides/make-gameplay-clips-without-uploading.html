Make gameplay clips without uploading your video

Updated 28 November 2026

Most online clip makers ask you to upload your whole recording first. For a long gameplay session that can mean gigabytes, a slow upload, a wait in a queue, and a copy of your footage on a server you don't control. There is another way.

Step by step

  1. Open the tool in your browser. Go to ReelMyGame in Chrome or Edge. There is nothing to install and no account to make.
  2. Choose your recording. Drop in an MP4, MOV or WebM file. Your browser reads it straight from your disk. No bytes of the video are sent to us.
  3. Let your computer do the work. Finding moments, cropping to 9:16, adding captions and making the final MP4 all happen on your own device, using your browser's built-in video tools.
  4. Save the finished clips. Download each clip straight from the page. Close the tab and nothing is left behind on any server.

Try it on your own recording. 3 free clips a month, no signup, and your video is never uploaded.

Why "never uploaded" matters

  • Unreleased games stay private. Playtests of a game that isn't out yet never leave your machine.
  • No waiting. A large file starts processing straight away instead of uploading first.
  • No storage limits. There is no upload size cap because nothing is uploaded.
  • Nothing to delete later. There is no copy of your footage on our servers to worry about.

What we do see

The page loads like any website, and if you log in or buy Pro we store your email and plan. We never receive your videos or clips. Read the full privacy policy.

Questions

How can it work without uploading?

Modern browsers can decode, analyse and encode video on your own computer. ReelMyGame uses those built-in features, so the whole job runs locally.

Does it work offline?

The page and the optional speech model need to load first. After that, the video work itself happens on your device.

Which browser should I use?

Chrome or Edge on a computer works best. Phones work for shorter videos.
