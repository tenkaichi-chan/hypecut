How to turn a stream VOD into Shorts and TikToks

Updated 28 November 2026

A two-hour stream usually has a handful of moments worth clipping, and finding them by hand means watching the whole thing again. Here is a faster way to turn a VOD into vertical clips for Shorts, TikTok and Reels.

Step by step

  1. Download your VOD as a video file. Most streaming platforms, including Twitch and YouTube Live, let you download past broadcasts as MP4. Your streaming software can also save a local recording while you stream. Either works.
  2. Drop the VOD into ReelMyGame. Open the free tool in Chrome or Edge on a computer and drop the file in. It stays on your computer: there is no upload, which matters when a VOD is several gigabytes.
  3. Find the loud, busy moments. Every second gets a score from loudness and on-screen motion. Chat-worthy moments, such as a clutch play, a jump scare or you yelling, tend to be both loud and busy, so they rise to the top.
  4. Turn on speech subtitles if you commentate. If you talk on stream, switch on speech subtitles (beta). It downloads a small speech model once and then writes word-by-word subtitles on your own device.
  5. Download the clips and post them. Each clip comes out as a 9:16 MP4 with captions burned in, ready for Shorts, TikTok and Reels.

Tips for clipping streams

  • Long VODs on a computer. Very long files are best handled in Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop. Phones work for shorter videos.
  • Keep your face cam in shot. If your webcam sits in a corner, the full-frame option (with a blurred fill) keeps the whole picture, webcam included.
  • Post clips the same week. Clips from last night's stream bring people to tonight's stream.
  • Link back. Put your channel link in the Short description so viewers can find the full stream.

Questions

Do I have to upload my VOD?

No. ReelMyGame runs in your browser and reads the file on your own computer. Your VOD is never uploaded.

How does it pick moments?

It scores each second by how loud it is and how much is moving on screen, then turns the highest spikes into clips. You can adjust the trims and titles before downloading.

Can I edit the captions?

Yes. Every clip has an editable hook caption, and speech subtitles can be edited before the clip is made.
