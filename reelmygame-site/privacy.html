Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

The short version

Your videos never leave your device. The clip tool runs entirely in your browser. We only collect what's needed to run Pro subscriptions.

Your videos

Recordings you add to the tool are read and processed by your own browser. They are not uploaded to our servers. If you turn on speech subtitles, a speech-recognition model is downloaded to your browser from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and runs on your device; your audio is not sent anywhere.

Pro purchases

Payments are processed by Gumroad. Gumroad collects your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card details. We check your license key with Gumroad to confirm Pro is active, and keep a count of how many Pro clips you've made this month. The key is also stored in your browser (and on your account if you're logged in) so Pro stays unlocked.

Accounts (optional)

You can use the free tool without an account. If you sign up, we store your email address, a securely hashed version of your password (never the password itself), your Pro key if you link one, and how many free clips you've made this month. A sign-in cookie keeps you logged in for up to 30 days; logging out removes it. If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares your email address with us to log you in; we don't get your Google password. Password-reset emails are sent from our Gmail account.

Hosting and cookies

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which may log basic technical data (such as IP address) for security. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The only cookie is the sign-in cookie, and only if you log in. We count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track individual visitors. Your browser's local storage keeps small settings, such as how many free clips you've made this month and your Pro key.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you at any time by emailing sehbag11@gmail.com.

Children

Pro purchases are for adults or must be made with a parent or guardian's permission.

See also our Terms of Service and Refund Policy.
